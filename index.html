00 — About me

I study how design shapes labor and technological futures.

I am an HCI and design researcher working across worker-centered design, service design, and critical inquiry. My research examines how platforms, automation, and design practices shape labor conditions, and how designers, workers, and institutions can build more equitable technological futures. I am particularly interested in labor and AI, justice-oriented design methods, citizen-centered automation, sustainable interaction, and design education.
